### 2a. `sourceCommit` is off by one commit, by construction — the staleness was over-stated

`generate:ownership` writes `sourceCommit = git rev-parse HEAD` at the moment the generator
runs. A maintainer runs the generator, *then* commits. So the value recorded is always the
**parent** of the commit that lands the artifact. Verified across three generations:

| Commit that landed `component-ownership.manifest.json` | `sourceCommit` it recorded | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| `e986952` | `8d80bc3` | its parent |
| `8d80bc3` | `4c9fb7a` | its parent |
| `4c9fb7a` | `be76ddb` | its parent |

Consequences, all verified:

- The ownership manifest read `8d80bc39` but its **content** was generated against the
  working tree that became `e986952` — it already contained `useAsyncOptions`,
  `useDualModel` and `useRevealAndFocus`, three composables that do not exist at `8d80bc39`.
- `quality-matrix.json` and `capability-matrix.json` do **not** compute their own binding —
  quality copies `manifest.sourceCommit`, capability copies `quality.sourceCommit`. The
  capability matrix was in fact last regenerated at `7984c68` (its per-row `componentCommit`
  values prove it: `DzCascader`, `DzFormField`, `DzFileUpload`, `DzAccordion` all read
  `e986952`), while wearing an `8d80bc39` label.
- So the true content drift was **not 15 commits**:

| Artifact | Label it carried | Content actually generated at | Real drift to HEAD |
|---|---|---|---|
| `component-ownership.manifest.json` | `8d80bc39` | `e986952` | 13 commits |
| `quality-matrix.json` | `8d80bc39` (copied) | `e986952` | 13 commits |
| `capability-matrix.json` | `8d80bc39` (copied) | `7984c68` | 12 commits |

- `git diff --name-only 7984c68..HEAD -- packages/core/src` is only **6 files**:
  `DzFormField.contract.spec.ts`, `DzFormField.types.ts`, `DzDialog.spec.ts`,
  `DzDialogContent.vue`, `useSidebar.ts`, `useSidebar.spec.ts`.
  `DzDialogContent` is a **compound part**, not a matrix row; `useSidebar` is a composable,
  not a row; the other four are specs and types. **Not one row source moved.** That is why
  the regeneration below is content-neutral, and it is a real answer, not a missing diff.

**Defect recorded (see §8, D1):** an artifact whose `sourceCommit` is the parent of its own
landing commit, and two artifacts that copy that label instead of computing their own,
violate `<evidence_rules>` ("a metric without a commit binding is not evidence") in the
subtler direction — the binding exists but points one commit early, and downstream matrices
inherit a binding that describes a different artifact's generation moment.
